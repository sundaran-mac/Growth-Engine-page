GROWTH DESK · FUNCTIONAL DOCUMENT · 12 OCT 2026 · JEEVA
Growth Desk: one app to plan, make, approve, publish, measure and sell, for every Humini product.
This page explains how the app works, in plain words, with the flow, the screens, who does what, what is automated, how it connects to each social channel, the data behind it, and what it will take to build. The clickable prototype is on the page “Growth Desk - app UI and prototype” (press Present on Home). It builds on the Growth Engine page, which set out the ten-stage process this app runs.

01 · WHAT IT IS
Claude drafts. People decide. Tools do the repeating.
Today the work is spread over Figma, a reel studio on one laptop, Drive, WhatsApp threads and spreadsheets. Nobody can see in one place what is planned, what waits for approval, what went out, or which post brought an install.
The problem
Marketing for one product lives in six tools and three people’s heads. Approvals happen in chat, so there is no record. No post is linked to an install, so nobody knows what works.
What changes
One place per product holds its truth. Claude drafts ideas, briefs, captions and scene files. The owner and Elan approve. Publishing, replies and numbers run on their own.
Who uses it
The product team does marketing and sales too: product owners, designers, developers, testers, plus Jeeva, Tamil, Sundaran, Elan and a community owner.
What it is not
Not a replacement for Figma, CapCut or Adobe: it hands work to them and collects the result. It never posts anything unapproved. No paid ads in version 1.
02 · THE FLOW IN ONE PICTURE
From a product’s repo to a signed partner, in ten stages and two gates.
Every product runs the same loop. Orange stages are made with Claude’s help, the black ones are human gates, green ones run on their own. The numbers on Analytics feed the next round of ideas every Monday.

Schedules and events
A job queue runs the timed work (Monday, nightly, publish times) and reacts to events (starred, frozen, approved). Every job is logged and can be retried.
Claude’s budget
Claude writes words, never pixels: ideas, briefs, captions, scene files, checks and notes. Renders run on our own machines and tools. One spend cap per product per month.
Fails safe
If a post fails, nothing retries silently: the owner is told, the piece goes back to Scheduled, and the channel is marked. Approvals never expire into “approved”.

09 · CHALLENGES, AND HOW WE MAKE IT HAPPEN
The hard parts are permissions, trust and people’s time, not code.
Each challenge has what makes it hard and what we do about it. Then the build: options, architecture, phases, team and the first version.
Platform access
WHY IT IS HARD
Publishing APIs need business accounts, app review and approval (LinkedIn especially). Rules change without notice.
WHAT WE DO
Start with the platforms’ own schedulers; add one connector at a time once approved. Sundaran checks each fact and dates it.
WhatsApp has no posting route for Channels
WHY IT IS HARD
The channel where installs happen most cannot be automated today, as far as we found.
WHAT WE DO
Reminder + ready file at the right time; Business Platform only for opt-in messages, with consent.
Claims and brand safety
WHY IT IS HARD
A wrong claim on a public post costs trust, and Apple has rejected our wording before.
WHAT WE DO
Product kit + never-say lint + two human gates. Nothing unapproved can be scheduled.
Consent and licences
WHY IT IS HARD
Real faces, stock footage, music and AI people all carry rules.
WHAT WE DO
Consent record per face; licence field per asset; AI label on by default when people are generated.
Privacy of our own data
WHY IT IS HARD
Leads are people and organisations; tokens give access to our accounts.
WHAT WE DO
Role-based access, secrets in a vault, audit log, minimum data on leads; follow PDPA (Singapore) and DPDP (India).
Attribution on iOS
WHY IT IS HARD
Installs are hard to tie to a post on the App Store.
WHAT WE DO
UTM on every link, App Store campaign links (check first), and in-app “how did you hear” as a backup.
Claude’s cost and quality
WHY IT IS HARD
Generating ideas and copy at volume costs tokens, and drafts can be wrong.
WHAT WE DO
Words only, never pixels; a monthly cap per product; every draft passes the lint and a human.
People’s time
WHY IT IS HARD
The product team already has a day job; marketing tasks slip.
WHAT WE DO
Named owner per stage, 24 h approval rule with a backup, a 30-minute Monday review, small weekly batches.
Too much, too soon
WHY IT IS HARD
A full app before a working process wastes effort.
WHAT WE DO
Run the process by hand first (phase 1), then build only what repeats.
Measuring what matters
WHY IT IS HARD
Views and likes are easy to collect and easy to over-read.
WHAT WE DO
The ladder and value score; the Monday note picks one thing to do more of and one to stop.
Three ways to build it
A
Assemble existing tools
Platform schedulers + a sheet + Drive + Claude projects + Figma. No code.
Fast to start · nothing to maintain · but no single view, no approvals record, no link from post to install.
B
Build Growth Desk in full
The app on this page, all modules, all connectors.
One place for everything · but months before it is useful, and built before we know what repeats.
C
Hybrid: A now, B in phases
RECOMMENDED
Run Live on A for six weeks; build Growth Desk’s core (kit, briefs, approvals, library, analytics) and switch module by module.
Useful in week 1 · each module replaces a manual step we already understand.
